        <section class="fm-side-box" aria-labelledby="datasets-title">
          <h2 id="datasets-title">Datasets</h2>
          <ul class="fm-dataset-list">
            <li><a href="https://data.gov/">U.S. Government Open Data</a><p>Open U.S. government data on health, energy, finance, and many other topics.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://archive.ics.uci.edu/">UCI Machine Learning Repository</a><p>A classic repository for machine learning datasets and model training.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/monitoring/climate-at-a-glance/">NOAA Climate at a Glance</a><p>Historical climate data by region, useful for environmental research, policy questions, and time-series practice.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://dados.gov.br/">Portal Brasileiro de Dados Abertos</a><p>Dados abertos do governo brasileiro para pesquisa, ensino e projetos aplicados.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://basedosdados.org/">Base dos Dados</a><p>Plataforma dedicada a universalizar o acesso a dados de alta qualidade no Brasil e no mundo.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://sites.google.com/view/manoel-bittencourt/resources">Manoel Bittencourt resources</a><p>Academic resource page with data and research links.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=8">CEPII Gravity Database</a><p>International trade data commonly used for gravity models and applied trade research.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://www.enterprisesurveys.org/en/enterprisesurveys">World Bank Enterprise Surveys</a><p>Firm-level survey data on the business environment across more than 160 economies.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://physionet.org/">PhysioNet</a><p>Medical and physiological datasets; some resources require institutional or approved research access.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://geosampa.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/PaginasPublicas/_SBC.aspx">GeoSampa</a><p>Plataforma da Prefeitura de S&atilde;o Paulo para visualizar e baixar dados urbanos e georreferenciados.</p></li>
            <li><a href="https://www.kaggle.com/">Kaggle</a><p>Public datasets and data science competitions for practice and teaching examples.</p></li>
          </ul>
